About S.V. Infra Projects

S.V. Infra Projects is a trusted real estate development company based in Hyderabad, Telangana state, India. The promoters bring over 20 years of extensive experience in the construction industry and have successfully executed several projects across residential gated communities for villas and apartments, commercial, and layout development sectors under previous ventures. Operating under the name S.V. Infra Projects for the past 8 years, the company has consistently delivered projects on schedule, adhering to high standards of quality, transparency, and statutory compliance, thereby earning long-term trust from clients. S.V. Infra Projects is known for quality workmanship, timely delivery, and client satisfaction.
